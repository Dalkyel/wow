Craft materials for profit

Compare buying ingredients and crafting a material with buying it ready-made. Includes known recipes that produce leather, hides, or inputs for another known recipe. All costs assume purchased ingredients, using the purchase prices in your profession’s material settings, even when their source is Gathered or Crafted. Each material names its best recipe to vendor, ranked by profit per final craft. Expand the comparison for costs, purchases, and crafting steps.

MaterialPurchases per craft Cost, savings & profit

Savings are a lower material cost, not sale income. Horde AH sales include a 5% selling fee on the sale price: total sale cost = crafting cost + selling fee; profit = sale price − total sale cost. The fee and total cost appear below each AH profit. Purchase prices do not receive an extra fee, and vendor sales have no AH fee. AH profit assumes selling at the snapshot minimum. Listing deposits are refunded on a successful sale; lost deposits from expired or canceled listings are not included. Auction fee reference. Listings do not guarantee a sale or enough stock at the minimum price. Missing prices stay Unknown. A cheaper material does not guarantee profit on the final item; check its recipe before buying. Material cost columns buy each ingredient directly. Recipe comparisons craft this material and any intermediate steps needed to reach the final item, buy the remaining ingredients, and include whole crafting batches. Leftovers are not counted as income.

Materials for my known recipes

Prices are per item. These are hypothetical purchase ceilings, not live price checks — go to a profession tab to see what's profitable at today's prices. Three scenarios per material: craft it once and sell the result to a vendor; craft it once and resell the result on the auction house (after the 5% fee); or craft it into something deeper that eventually sells to a vendor (any number of crafting steps). Each scenario keeps other ingredients at their cheapest available cost (bought, crafted, or gathered) regardless of what you've selected elsewhere. The recipe ceiling range is shown underneath each one; only the recipe with the highest ceiling can profit at the maximum. Expand the third scenario's comparison to see all purchases against one shared budget. Unknown limits and recipes that cannot profit even with this material free are excluded.
